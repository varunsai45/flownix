08 / Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Straight answers.

New to digital marketing? Start here. If something is still unclear, ask us and we'll explain it without jargon.

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency helps people discover your business online. That can include building your website, running ads, improving your Google visibility, creating social media content and measuring which activities bring enquiries.

Which service should I start with?

It depends on your goal. If customers need a place to learn about you, start with a website. If you need enquiries sooner, paid ads may help. If you want to improve unpaid Google visibility over time, consider SEO. We can help you choose after understanding your business and budget.

How much do Flownix services cost?

Small, clearly defined projects may start at ₹1,000. Business websites commonly fall in the ₹10,000–₹30,000 range, while larger websites and connected systems can be scoped up to ₹1,00,000. Monthly marketing management can start around ₹8,000–₹20,000, excluding ad spend. Final pricing depends on the work agreed.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?

No, not by default. The agency management fee and the money paid to Google, Facebook or Instagram are separate. We explain both amounts before a campaign starts.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO usually takes time. Progress depends on your website, competition, location, content and technical health. No honest agency can guarantee a number-one Google position or an exact timeline.

Can Flownix help automate our leads and follow-ups?

Yes. Depending on your tools and process, we can help organise leads in a CRM, create reminders, connect forms and messaging tools, and automate repetitive steps. We agree on what is possible before building it.

Do you work only with Bangalore businesses?

Flownix is based in Bangalore, India, and can work remotely with businesses in other locations too. The right setup depends on the service and the project.
