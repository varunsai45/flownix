Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Flownix website or contact us about a project. Flownix is an independent digital agency run by Varun Sai, based in Bangalore, India.

Information we handleHow enquiries workService providersYour choicesContact

1. Information we handle

If you contact us, you may provide your name, business name, email address, phone number, service interests, approximate budget, project description and any other information in your message. Provide only what is needed to discuss your project. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal documents through the enquiry draft.

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, device or browser information and request times to deliver and secure the website. These hosting records are separate from the enquiry form.

2. How the enquiry form works

The current form prepares a draft in your browser. Clicking Prepare enquiry does not submit it to Flownix or a form database. The website does not save the draft to its own local storage or a server.

If you choose Open email app or Open WhatsApp, the draft is passed to that app or service. Its provider may process the draft when you open it, even before you send the message. You must press Send in that app for the enquiry to reach us.

The Copy enquiry button copies the draft to your device's clipboard when permission is available. Your browser, email app or device may separately retain autofill entries, clipboard contents or drafts under its own settings. Once you send an enquiry, a copy may be retained in our email or messaging account.

3. How we use information

We use information you send to respond to your questions, discuss your requirements, prepare proposals, communicate about agreed work and maintain necessary project or business records. We may also use relevant records to address misuse, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations.

This website does not include a newsletter signup, advertising audience collection or an automatic marketing subscription. Sending a project enquiry does not sign you up to one.

4. Hosting, email, messaging and external websites

The site is hosted on Vercel. Our published contact email uses Gmail, and WhatsApp links open WhatsApp. These providers process information under their own policies and may operate infrastructure outside India.

  • Vercel Privacy Notice
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy

We may disclose information when needed to provide an agreed service, comply with applicable law or protect legal rights. Project-specific access to additional tools or collaborators should be addressed in the project arrangements.

Portfolio links take you to independently operated client websites. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit them.

5. Cookies and analytics

The current website code does not set tracking cookies, include advertising pixels or install analytics scripts. It does not store enquiry entries in localStorage or sessionStorage. Hosting security features, your browser and external services may have their own cookie or storage behaviour. If we introduce additional tracking or a hosted form service, we will update this policy and provide any choices required by applicable law.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry and project communications for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose they were collected, ongoing work, record-keeping or applicable legal obligations. Retention can differ between an initial enquiry and an active client project, and service providers may maintain their own backups or logs.

We aim to protect information using appropriate account and access safeguards. No website, email or messaging service can guarantee complete security. Contact us if you believe information has been sent to us in error.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse without preparing an enquiry. The business, phone, service and budget fields are optional; you can also contact us directly instead of using the form.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent us, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or raise a privacy concern, contact Varun Sai using the details below. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider your request under applicable law and explain if some records must be retained. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

Please contact us first with concerns so we can try to resolve them. This does not restrict any complaint or other rights you may have under applicable law.

8. Children

Flownix's services and enquiry flow are intended for adults seeking business services. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. We will provide additional notice where applicable law requires it.
